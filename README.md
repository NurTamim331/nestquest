# Nest Quest — Verified Smart Property Marketplace (Bangladesh)

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-Vercel%20Production-16a34a?style=for-the-badge&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://nestquest-five.vercel.app/)

🌐 **Live URL**: [https://nestquest-five.vercel.app/](https://nestquest-five.vercel.app/)

**Nest Quest** is a comprehensive web-based real estate platform tailored for Bangladesh's property ecosystem. It solves critical market pain points—fake listings, opaque pricing, land record (*Khatian / Mutation / Namjari*) confusion, and unverified intermediaries—by combining verified property data, interactive GIS smart maps, AI price estimation, legal support, fraud detection, and group buying (co-ownership) in one unified platform.

## ✨ Implemented Features (100% Aligned with SRS Chapter 4 & Chapter 8 Figma Designs)

1. **Landing & Home Hub** — Forest green hero banner, verified search bar, live platform metrics (`5000+ Properties`, `100% Verified`, `200+ Legal Experts`, `98% Success Rate`), and interactive **AI Budget & Area Recommendation Engine**.
2. **Buyer Property Search, Filter & Side-by-Side Comparison** — Filter by Dhaka zones (`Gulshan`, `Banani`, `Dhanmondi`, `Uttara`, `Bashundhara`), property type, price in Crore/Lac BDT, verification status, and group-buying eligibility.
3. **Property Details & 3-Step Legal Escrow Checkout** — Photo gallery, verification reliability score bar (`92%+`), AI fair market valuation range, nearby hospitals/police/schools map, flood/crime/noise hazard report, encrypted legal document log, and 3-step buyer checkout.
4. **Interactive Smart Map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap)** — 100% free GIS map with clickable property pins, AI Price Estimation & Risk Assessment popups, flood/safety zone overlays, and hospital/police POI markers.
5. **Group Buying (Co-Ownership) & Group Chats** — Select or create co-investment groups (`Dhaka Property Investors`, `Gulshan Elite Group`, `Banani Investment Circle`), fractional share allocation, automated draft co-ownership agreement, member resolution voting, and group chat.
6. **Create Sell Post & Encrypted Document Upload** — Upload property photos and sensitive land records (*CS/RS/BS Khatian, Mutation/Namjari, Title Deed, Khajna Tax Receipt*) directly into the Admin & Lawyer verification queue.
7. **Lawyer Directory, Profile & `Nest Quest Legal` Portal** — Browse Bar Council verified advocates (`Advocate Rafiqul Islam`, `Advocate Nasrin Ahmed`, etc.), book consultations, manage `Today's Appointments`, and approve pending Khatian/Namjari documents.
8. **Property Advisors Directory, Chat & 6-Stage Deal Flow Tracker** — Connect with licensed advisors (`Dr. Karim Ahmed`, `Farida Rahman`, `Karim Hassan`), chat in real time, and advance deals across `Selected → Legal Check → Negotiation → Payment → Registration → Handover`.
9. **User Profile (`Nur Uddin Tamim`)** — Verified NID badge, bank escrow settings, saved wishlist, quick actions, and transaction history table.
10. **Admin Dashboard & Fraud Detection Engine (`Mohammad Rahman`)** — Monitor platform KPIs, approve/reject pending seller posts, and block suspicious price anomalies detected by the Fraud Detection module.
